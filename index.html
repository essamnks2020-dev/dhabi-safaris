01 The approach

A local operator, not a middleman.

We handle the driving, the routing, and the park logistics ourselves. Nothing about your trip is subcontracted out to someone we have never met.

02 Who we are

Based in Arusha, close to the ground.

Dhabi Safaris & Holidays is based in Arusha — the town every Northern Circuit safari passes through — and we have kept our operation close to the ground on purpose.

We do not book your hotel rooms for you. What we do is get you there and back safely, with a guide who knows which turn-off leads to the crater rim at the right hour, and who can read the bush well enough to know where the migration actually is this week.

03 The Northern Circuit

Four parks, one road out of Arusha.

Each stop is a different Tanzania — open plains, a crater floor, elephant country, flamingo lakes. Routes are typically combined into multi-day itineraries.

01 / 04
01

Serengeti

Endless plains and, in season, the Great Migration — the centrepiece of most itineraries.

≈ 6–7 hrs from Arusha
02

Ngorongoro Crater

A collapsed volcanic caldera holding one of the densest concentrations of wildlife on the continent.

≈ 2.5–3 hrs from Arusha
03

Tarangire

Baobab country, and among the highest elephant densities anywhere in Africa.

≈ 1.5–2 hrs from Arusha
04

Lake Manyara

A soda lake beneath the Rift Valley escarpment, known for tree-climbing lions and flamingos.

≈ 1.5 hrs from Arusha
04 On the road

Our vehicles, our mechanics, our routes.

We run and service our own safari vehicles rather than hiring per trip — it is how we keep breakdown risk low on long game-drive days.

4×4 pop-top vehicles

Raised roof for game viewing and photography, built for the terrain between parks, not adapted from city cars.

Guided at your pace

Departure times built around wildlife activity, not a fixed group schedule.

Serviced between trips

Our own workshop services the fleet after each safari, before the next one goes out.
